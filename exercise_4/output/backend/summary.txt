1. **Backend Development Overview**: It focuses on the technology that manages the “behind-the-scenes” functionality of websites and applications, involving servers, databases, and APIs.

2. **Role of Servers**: Servers store, process, and manage data, ensuring fast and reliable online experiences by handling requests and updating databases.

3. **Importance of Databases**: Databases safely store information, enabling easy access and organization of data when needed.

4. **Function of APIs**: APIs enable communication between servers and client-side applications, facilitating efficient data retrieval and interaction.

5. **Backend's Impact**: It ensures that websites and applications function seamlessly, enhancing digital experiences by connecting the necessary technological components.
